Executive Summary: Debunking the 11% Claim

Reconstructed 12 months of operational data across borrowers, accounts, agents, calls, digital events, and payments. The reported 11% improvement between February and March was proven to be an illusion caused by 28 vs 31 calendar days (+10.71%). Daily recovery remained completely flat at ₹6.2M/day.

Data Forensics Summary

  • Duplicate Payments: 500 duplicate IDs removed; ₹575.8M of FAILED/PENDING/REVERSED records excluded.
  • Ghost Attribution: 79.8% of recovered funds had no operational touch within 7 days.
  • Telephony Inactive Routing: 60.1% calls routed to inactive vendor gateways.
  • Regulatory Hours: 50.1% calls dialed outside RBI hours (8am-7pm IST) due to unnormalized UTC timestamps.
  • Simpson's Paradox: Recovery rate is flat (~12.5% - 13.5%) across all DPD brackets and loan products.
  • Counterfactual: Targeted vs untargeted accounts show zero uplift (p = 0.42).
